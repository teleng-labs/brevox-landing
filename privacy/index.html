Privacy Policy

100% on-device. No Brevox servers. No tracking.

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Overview

Brevox is designed as a private voice-first notebook. Audio, transcripts, summaries, tags, and notes are processed and stored on your device.

Data Brevox does not collect

Brevox does not collect or transmit your recordings, transcripts, notes, folders, tags, usage history, advertising identifiers, or personal tracking data. Operational events used inside the app stay in memory and are not sent to Brevox or third-party analytics services.

No Brevox servers

Brevox does not operate backend servers for your notes. The app does not upload your voice notes to Brevox infrastructure for processing, storage, analytics, or review.

On-device processing

Voice capture and note processing are intended to happen on your Apple devices. Your content remains under your control through the device, iOS, watchOS, and your Apple account settings.

Apple services

App Store distribution, TestFlight, crash reports, speech recognition, and operating system features may be handled by Apple depending on your device settings. Apple processes that information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Support emails

If you contact support, your email address and message are used only to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive recordings or private note content in support emails.

Children

Brevox is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information, email support so the message can be deleted.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Brevox evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
