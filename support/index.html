Support

Need help with Brevox?

Email support and include your device model, iOS/watchOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Email support

Does Brevox upload my recordings?

No. Brevox is designed to process and store your notes on-device, without Brevox servers.

Do I need an account?

No Brevox account is required for the current product direction. The app is built around local use on your Apple devices.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Apple Watch capture is part of the product: record quickly, then review and refine from iPhone.

What does "85% audio, 15% pen" mean?

Brevox prioritizes speaking as the fastest input, while keeping enough manual editing for titles, corrections, tags, and context.

How do I join TestFlight?

Use the email button on this page or the home page. You will receive instructions when a beta slot is available.

Can support recover deleted notes?

No. Because Brevox does not store your notes on its own servers, support cannot recover local content deleted from your device.
